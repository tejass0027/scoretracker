Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use ScoreTracker without an internet connection?
Yes! ScoreTracker is built as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). Once loaded, you can track matches anywhere—even at remote grounds without Wi-Fi or mobile network.
How does the Tournament / League Engine work?
Tournament mode allows up to 10 teams in a round-robin schedule. It automatically calculates matches played, won, lost, points, and Net Run Rate (NRR), and crowns champions through grand playoff matches.
Can I save and export my match scorecards?
Absolutely. All games are automatically saved locally and can also be saved to your Google Cloud Vault, or exported as JSON backups to share with coaches, teammates, or tournament organizers.
Which sports are supported?
ScoreTracker currently supports 14 sports: Cricket, Football, Basketball, Tennis, Badminton, Hockey, Volleyball, Baseball, Rugby, Kabaddi, Table Tennis, Golf, Boxing, and MMA.
